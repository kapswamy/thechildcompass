Privacy Policy

The Child Compass (Pediatric & Neonatal Clinic) · Vikarabad, Telangana
Effective & Last Updated: September 2026

At The Child Compass, we are committed to protecting the privacy and personal health information of children, parents, and guardians who access our clinic services, website, and mobile applications. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, handle, store, and safeguard your data.

🛡️ Our Fundamental Privacy Principle We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal health information or phone number with third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstance.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our clinic via our website or mobile application, we may collect the following information:

  • Contact & Identification Details: Parent or guardian full name, mobile telephone number, and optional email address.
  • Child Details for Care Coordination: Child's first name, age or date of birth, and consultation reason (e.g., newborn screening, vaccination schedule, developmental evaluation).
  • Appointment Preferences: Preferred consultation day, timing, and clinic location.
  • Clinic Communications: Messages or inquiries sent via WhatsApp or telephone directly to our front desk.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data collected strictly for clinical and operational purposes:

  • To schedule, confirm, and coordinate pediatric appointments with Dr. Vishnuvardhan K.
  • To send important appointment reminders, vaccination due dates, and clinic schedule updates.
  • To respond to parent inquiries regarding pediatric clinic services and screening tests.
  • To improve the usability, accessibility, and navigation of our clinic application.

3. Parent Guide AI Assistant & Educational Inquiries

Our platform includes a Parent Education Guide designed to assist parents with general pediatric health milestones, nutrition questions, and newborn guidelines.

  • Educational Purposes Only: The AI guide provides evidence-based educational insights and does not provide clinical diagnoses, prescriptions, or emergency triage.
  • No Medical Records Storage: Questions typed into the Parent Guide assistant are processed transiently via secure serverless infrastructure and are not attached to an identified medical record.
  • Parent Anonymity: We advise parents not to submit sensitive personally identifiable medical records in open AI chat prompts.

4. Data Storage, Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

  • All web and mobile communications are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Appointment records are retained in compliance with applicable Indian medical council standards and clinical establishment guidelines.
  • You may at any time request that your contact details be removed from our appointment reminder communications.

5. Children’s Privacy Disclosures

Our services and applications are intended for use by parents, legal guardians, and adult caregivers (aged 18 and older) seeking medical guidance and appointment bookings for their children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 without parental consent.

6. Third-Party Services & Links

Our application may provide links or integration with trusted third-party services, including:

  • WhatsApp: For direct communication with our front desk (subject to WhatsApp's privacy policies).
  • Google Maps: To help parents navigate to our Vikarabad clinic location.
  • Phone Call Integration: To initiate direct telephone calls to the clinic reception.
